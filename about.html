ABOUT REVYU

Study Smarter.
Focus Better.

REVYU is a simple study and productivity tool designed to help students manage their time, stay focused, and build better study habits.

01 — REVYU

What is REVYU?

REVYU is a study-focused website that helps students organize their study sessions through time management and focused work periods.

Instead of studying for long periods without breaks, REVYU encourages students to work in focused sessions followed by short breaks.

02 — TECHNIQUE

The Pomodoro Technique

The Pomodoro Technique is a time-management method that divides work into focused intervals separated by short breaks.

A traditional Pomodoro session consists of 25 minutes of focused work, followed by a 5-minute break.
